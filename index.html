About Me

I am a PhD student in the Department of Mathematics at the Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur under the supervision of Prof. Ratna Dutta.

My research interests lie in public-key cryptography, particularly functional encryption, lattice-based cryptography, code-based cryptography, and post-quantum cryptography.

I am interested in designing efficient cryptographic constructions and studying their mathematical foundations through algebra, number theory, coding theory, and combinatorics.
