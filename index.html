About

About Me

I am a PhD student in the Department of Mathematics at the Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur under the supervision of Prof. Ratna Dutta.

Previously, I completed my post-graduation from Ramakrishna Mission Vivekananda Educational and Research Institute (RKMVERI), Belur, in Pure Mathematics. I completed my graduation from Midnapore College (Autonomous) with Honours in Mathematics.

Research Interests

My research interests lie in public-key cryptography, particularly in the design and analysis of modern cryptographic primitives.
