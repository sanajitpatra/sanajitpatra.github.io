About Me

I am a PhD student in the Department of Mathematics at the Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur under the supervision of Prof. Ratna Dutta.

My research interests lie in modern public-key cryptography, with particular emphasis on Functional Encryption, Lattice-Based Cryptography, Code-Based Cryptography, and Post-Quantum Cryptography.

I am interested in developing efficient cryptographic constructions and exploring their mathematical foundations through algebra, number theory, coding theory, and combinatorics.

My recent work includes research on laconic encryption, registration-based encryption, identity-based encryption, anamorphic cryptography, and code-based cryptographic primitives.
