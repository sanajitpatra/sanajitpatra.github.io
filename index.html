About Me

I am a PhD student in the Department of Mathematics at the Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur under the supervision of Prof. Ratna Dutta.

My research interests include Functional Encryption, Lattice-Based Cryptography, Code-Based Cryptography, Post-Quantum Cryptography, and Anamorphic Cryptography.

I am interested in designing efficient cryptographic constructions and studying their mathematical foundations using tools from algebra, number theory, coding theory, and combinatorics.
