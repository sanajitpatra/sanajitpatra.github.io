Research Direction

Cryptography

My work focuses on the study and development of cryptographic constructions, with an emphasis on encryption schemes and cryptographic techniques that provide strong security guarantees.

Particular attention is given to functional encryption, lattice-based and code-based approaches, as well as emerging areas such as anamorphic cryptography and laconic encryption.
