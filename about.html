About Me

I am a PhD student in the Department of Mathematics at the Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur under the supervision of Prof. Ratna Dutta.

My research interests lie in public-key cryptography, particularly functional encryption, registration-based encryption, anamorphic cryptography, lattice-based cryptography and post-quantum cryptography.
